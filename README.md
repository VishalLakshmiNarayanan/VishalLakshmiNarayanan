## E-Commerce Conversion & Revenue Optimization (GCP BigQuery)

**Project Background**
Audit of 2,336 unique user events to map the customer journey and identify friction points within the sales funnel. This analysis transformed raw event logs into business-ready metrics to provide a roadmap for resource allocation.

**Executive Summary**
* **Technical Health:** Identified high-velocity checkout infrastructure with 93% efficiency from payment to purchase.
* **Attribution Efficiency:** Discovered Email as the primary driver of high-intent traffic (33% conversion) compared to Social Media (7%).
* **Unit Economics:** Established an Average Order Value (AOV) of $107.78 and defined a $40 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) limit to maintain profitability.

**Stack:** GCP, BigQuery, SQL, Looker, Funnel Analysis

## Tail Risk Management: Actuarial Ensemble Framework

**Project Background**
Development of a Risk-Aware Meta-Ensemble designed to diagnose and mitigate insurance tail-risk failure modes. The framework shifts modeling objectives from average accuracy to Tail Coverage (TCR) and Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) optimization.

**Executive Summary**
* **Tail Detection:** Tripled the detection rate for extreme-tail events at the 95% threshold (improving from 0.83% to 2.47%).
* **Residual Accuracy:** Achieved a 10.25% improvement in CVaR(95%) residuals, enabling more precise capital reserve allocation.
* **Solvency Protection:** Improved the Tail Coverage Ratio (TCR) @ 95% from 0.165 to 0.306, capturing nearly double the risk volume compared to traditional models.

**Stack:** Python, Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, Quantile Regression, Actuarial Science
